# Privacy

KareMetin is designed to work offline.

- Media, audio, subtitles, projects, presets, and rendered exports remain on the user's computer.
- On first launch, KareMetin downloads the Whisper model files from Hugging Face and stores them locally. It does not send transcription audio or text to a remote service.
- KareMetin contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising SDK, account system, or cloud synchronization.
- The initial model download requires an internet connection. After the model is downloaded, normal editing, transcription, and export do not require an internet connection.

Users remain responsible for the media and personal data they choose to process, store, share, or export.

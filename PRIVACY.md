# Privacy

KareMetin is designed to work offline.

- Media, audio, subtitles, projects, presets, and rendered exports remain on the user's computer.
- The bundled Whisper model runs locally. It does not send transcription audio or text to a remote service.
- KareMetin contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising SDK, account system, or cloud synchronization.
- The application may open links only when the user explicitly requests an external page. Normal editing, transcription, and export do not require an internet connection.

Users remain responsible for the media and personal data they choose to process, store, share, or export.
